Frequently Asked Questions

What is Retatrutide (LY3437943)?

Retatrutide (LY3437943) is a synthetic 39-amino-acid peptide investigated for activity at GLP-1, GIP and glucagon receptors. Its parent-peptide identifiers are CAS 2381089-83-2, molecular formula C221H342N46O68 and molecular weight approximately 4,731 g/mol. These specifications exclude counterions and formulation additives. Materials are supplied for laboratory research and analytical use only.
