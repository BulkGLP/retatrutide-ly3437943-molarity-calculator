For laboratory research reference only; not for human or veterinary use. No dosing, clinical advice, safety assessment, or independent wet-lab validation is provided.

Every assigned molar mass is a declared approximate average molar mass convention for the anhydrous parent species described by its cited reference. It is not a lot-specific measured molecular mass. Salts, counterions, hydrates, residual moisture and excipients are excluded from the parent-mass model.

Theoretical molarity assumes the nominal mass equals net parent-peptide mass and uses final solution volume, not solvent volume added. mg/mL = mass (mg) / final volume (mL); mM = mg/mL × 1000 / molar mass (g/mol); uM = mM × 1000. Decimal places are computational precision, not measurement accuracy.

The 1.0, 1.5, 2.0 and 2.5 mL scenarios are arithmetic only. They do not establish solubility, stability, pH, handling suitability, or container capacity, particularly for bulk powders. No single molar mass is assigned to mixtures or unresolved identities.

COA values are transcriptions of supplier-hosted documents for the submitted samples, not new measurements or certification of every sold lot. Chromatographic area purity is not net peptide content, sterility, or proof of absence of excipients. Original method labels are preserved; HPLC-UV is not silently relabeled RP-HPLC. Received, reported and test dates have separate fields.
